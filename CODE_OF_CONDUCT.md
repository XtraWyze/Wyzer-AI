# Code of Conduct

Wyzer aims to be a welcoming project for people of all experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus feedback on the work, not the person.
- Respect privacy and do not share another person's data without permission.
- Assume good intent while discussing bugs and design choices.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information, credentials, or sensitive logs.
- Deliberately disruptive behavior in issues, discussions, or pull requests.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed and may lead
to content removal or a participation ban when appropriate.
